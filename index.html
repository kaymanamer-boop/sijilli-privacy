Sijilli - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Sijilli ("we", "our", "the app") is a document tracking and renewal reminder application designed for UAE residents. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) — Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address, name, phone number (optional), selected emirate
  • Document data: Document types, reference numbers, expiry dates, and optional scanned images that you choose to add
  • Family/employee members: Names and document details you add for tracking
  • Usage data: AI chat interactions (message count and token usage for rate limiting)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide document expiry tracking and renewal reminders
  • To personalize renewal guides based on your emirate
  • To power the AI assistant with context about your documents
  • To enforce subscription limits and rate limiting

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS) with Row-Level Security (RLS) — you can only access your own data
  • Document scans are stored in a private storage bucket accessible only to the document owner
  • All communication is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS
  • The app enforces session timeouts after 5 minutes of inactivity
  • Screenshots and screen recording are blocked to protect sensitive data
  • Clipboard data containing sensitive information is automatically cleared after 60 seconds

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and file storage
  • Anthropic (Claude AI): Powers the AI assistant and document scanning. Your messages are processed but not stored by Anthropic for training purposes
  • Apple/Google: In-app purchase processing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any other third parties.

6. Your Rights (UAE PDPL)

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time
  • Data portability: Request your data in a portable format

7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data, documents, family members, and scanned images are permanently deleted.

8. Children's Privacy

Sijilli is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your rights, contact us at:
Email: privacy@sijilli.app
